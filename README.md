# Awesome AI Writing Tools

This is a curated list of tools for rewriting text, checking grammar, adjusting tone, drafting with AI, and checking whether text appears to be written by AI. A tool is added when it is live, maintained, and has an official homepage or repository. Entries are grouped by category and listed in alphabetical order.

## Rewriters and Paraphrasers

- [Rephrase AI](https://www.rephraseai.com) - Rewrites text in a chosen tone, with Academic, Professional, Casual and Creative profiles, and keeps the meaning the same.
- [Scribbr Paraphrasing Tool](https://www.scribbr.com/paraphrasing-tool/) - A free paraphrasing tool for students and writers, powered by QuillBot, that rephrases sentences and paragraphs.
- [Spinbot](https://spinbot.com) - A free text rewriting tool that rewords articles and paragraphs into new phrasing.
- [Trinka Paraphrasing Tool](https://www.trinka.ai/paraphrasing-tool) - An AI paraphrasing tool from Trinka for rephrasing sentences in academic and professional writing.
- [Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com) - An AI writing assistant that paraphrases text and offers rewrite suggestions in casual or formal tones.
- [Wordvice AI](https://www.wordvice.ai) - A writing assistant with paraphrasing, proofreading, and other revision tools.

## Grammar and Spelling

- [Ginger](https://www.gingersoftware.com) - An English grammar and writing app that checks text and suggests rephrasings and synonyms.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - A writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, clarity, and tone, and can suggest paragraph rewrites.
- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org) - A grammar, spelling, and punctuation checker for more than 30 languages that can run online or on your own server.
- [Linguix](https://linguix.com) - A grammar checker and writing app that checks grammar, punctuation, and style in several languages.
- [Outwrite](https://www.outwrite.com) - An AI writing assistant that checks spelling, grammar, and punctuation, and can help improve style and structure.
- [PerfectIt](https://www.perfectit.com) - Proofreading software that finds consistency issues such as spelling variants, capitalization, and hyphenation.
- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com) - A grammar and style editor with detailed reports for long documents, including fiction.
- [Revise AI](https://www.revise.net/) - An AI writing editor that shows grammar, clarity, and style suggestions as tracked changes you accept or reject.
- [Sapling](https://sapling.ai) - A language toolkit for teams, with grammar and spelling checks, autocomplete, paraphrasing, and AI detection.
- [Scribens](https://www.scribens.com) - A free grammar and spelling checker with extensions for browsers and common writing apps.
- [Trinka](https://www.trinka.ai) - An AI writing and grammar checker used for academic and professional documents.

## Tone and Style

- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com) - A fiction writing platform with tools to plan, write, and polish stories, including style and manuscript feedback.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) - Helps you make writing clearer by highlighting issues, and can check grammar and adjust tone.
- [Ludwig](https://ludwig.guru) - A writing editor that suggests edits and shows how phrases are used in published texts.
- [Markup AI](https://markup.ai) - Checks writing against brand, quality, and compliance standards so teams can review content before they publish.
- [Readable](https://readable.com) - A readability checker that scores text with common readability tests and flags style issues.
- [StyleWriter](https://www.editorsoftware.com) - Copy editing software for Microsoft Word that checks writing for plain English, clarity, and readability.
- [The Writer's Diet](https://writersdiet.com) - A style test and a Microsoft Word add in that flags wordy or weak patterns in your prose.
- [WordRake](https://www.wordrake.com) - Editing software for Microsoft Word and Outlook that suggests changes for brevity and simplicity.

## AI Writing Assistants

- [Anyword](https://www.anyword.com) - An AI writing tool for marketing copy that scores and suggests changes based on performance data.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - A conversational AI assistant from OpenAI that can draft, edit, and discuss text.
- [Claude](https://claude.com) - An AI assistant from Anthropic that can generate text, edit drafts, and brainstorm ideas.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) - Google's AI assistant, which can help with writing, planning, and brainstorming.
- [HyperWrite](https://www.hyperwriteai.com) - An AI writing assistant with autocomplete, web search, and citations, plus a document editor for drafting and rewriting.
- [Jenni](https://jenni.ai) - An AI research and academic writing assistant for essays, papers, and citations.
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.com) - Microsoft's AI assistant for creating, brainstorming, and solving problems in everyday work.
- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai) - AI features inside Notion for generating, searching, and editing text in your workspace.
- [Novelcrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com) - A novel writing workspace with a story bible, and optional AI help for outlining, writing, and review.
- [Paperpal](https://paperpal.com) - An AI academic writing and research workspace with language checks, paraphrasing, and submission tools.
- [Sudowrite](https://sudowrite.com) - An AI writing partner made for fiction, including help with plotting, drafting, and revision.
- [Writefull](https://www.writefull.com) - AI writing and proofreading tools for academic writing, including language checks and paraphrasing.

## AI Content Detectors

- [Compilatio](https://www.compilatio.net/en/) - An academic integrity platform with plagiarism checking and AI writing detection for teachers and students.
- [Copyleaks](https://copyleaks.com) - An AI and plagiarism detection platform for checking whether text appears original or written by AI.
- [GPTZero](https://gptzero.me) - An AI detector that scores text and can highlight sentences that appear to be written by AI.
- [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai) - An AI detector for checking whether text appears to have been written by models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.
- [Pangram](https://www.pangram.com) - An AI detector for checking whether text in essays, articles, and documents appears to have been written by AI.
- [Sapling AI Detector](https://sapling.ai/ai-content-detector) - A detector that scores a whole text and each sentence for the chance it was written by AI.
- [Scribbr](https://www.scribbr.com/ai-detector/) - A free AI detector that flags passages as human written, AI written, or revised with AI.
- [Turnitin](https://www.turnitin.com/solutions/topics/ai-writing/) - An academic integrity service that includes checks for AI writing in student work.
- [Winston AI](https://gowinston.ai) - An AI content detector and plagiarism checker for text that may have been written by tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

## MCP Servers and Developer Tools

- [alex](https://alexjs.com) - A command line linter that flags insensitive or inconsiderate phrasing and suggests alternatives.
- [LTeX+](https://ltex-plus.github.io/ltex-plus/) - An offline grammar and spelling checker that uses LanguageTool in editors, with support for LaTeX, Markdown, and other markup formats.
- [proselint](https://codeberg.org/amperser/proselint) - A linter for prose that checks style, typography, and wording issues.
- [Rephrase AI MCP Server](https://github.com/rephraseai/rephraseai-mcp) - A hosted MCP server for rewriting text in a chosen tone. It needs a Rephrase AI account.
- [Sapling MCP Server](https://sapling.ai/mcp) - A hosted MCP server that gives assistants access to Sapling tools for grammar checks, paraphrasing, and AI detection.
- [textlint](https://textlint.org) - An open source text linter for natural language, with plugins for Markdown and other formats.
- [Vale](https://vale.sh) - A markup aware linter that turns a style guide into checks you can run in an editor, on the command line, or through an official MCP server.
- [write-good](https://github.com/btford/write-good) - A linter for English prose.

## Open Source and Self Hosted Tools

- [FocusWriter](https://gottcode.org/focuswriter/) - A simple word processor that hides its interface so you can write without distraction, with daily goals and spell checking.
- [Grammalecte](https://www.grammalecte.net/) - An open source grammar and typography checker for French, with add ons for LibreOffice, Firefox, and other apps.
- [Harper](https://writewithharper.com) - A free, private, open source grammar checker that runs on your device.
- [Manuskript](https://www.theologeek.ch/manuskript/) - An open source writing tool for planning and drafting novels, including character, plot, and world notes.
- [novelWriter](https://novelwriter.io) - An open source plain text editor for novels, using a format similar to Markdown to organize many smaller documents.
- [Zettlr](https://www.zettlr.com) - An open source writing app for Markdown documents, with citation support, aimed at researchers, journalists, and writers.

## Contributing

Additions are welcome when they meet the rules below.

- Open one pull request for each tool.
- Link the official homepage or the official repository. Affiliate links are not accepted.
- Write one short, neutral sentence about what the tool does.
- The tool must be live and maintained.
- Keep the category in alphabetical order.
- If you work on the tool or would benefit from the listing, say so in the pull request.
